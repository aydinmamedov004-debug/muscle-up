Muscle Up

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

Muscle Up is built to work almost entirely on your phone. This page explains, plainly, the handful of places your information does leave the device — and the much longer list of places it doesn't.

What we collect

Four categories of information touch our systems (Firebase) or Google's AI service. Everything else about your training lives only on your device.

Account
Your email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication so you can sign in — or, if you use the app as a guest, an anonymous Firebase identifier with no personal information attached. We never see your password in plain text.
Profile
Age, weight, height, experience level, and weekly goal, entered during onboarding. Stored on your device to personalize the app.
Workout history
Programs, exercises, sets, reps, weights, and completed workouts — stored locally on your device only. Nothing here is uploaded or synced, unless you use the AI Coach below.
AI Coach chats
What you type to the Coach, plus context to make its advice relevant — your name, active program, exercise list, workout count, most recent workout, and profile details — is sent to Google's Gemini model to generate a reply.

How the AI Coach works

Every message you send to the in-app Coach is processed by Google's Firebase AI (Gemini) service, subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms. We don't store your conversations on our own servers, and we don't use them for anything beyond generating that one reply.

Guest mode

Using the app as a guest creates an anonymous Firebase account with no email, password, or personal information attached — it exists only to let the app function. Your workout data stays on your device exactly as it would with a full account, but it is tied to this device only: uninstalling the app or losing the device means losing that data, since there is no account to recover it from. Creating a full account at any time attaches your email/password to that same identifier without moving or duplicating any data.

What we don't collect

No hidden categories, no advertising SDKs, no analytics tracking you across apps.

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or camera
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or crash tracking

Retention & deletion

Delete your workout and program data anytime from Settings → Data — Clear Workout History or Delete All Programs — or by uninstalling the app. To delete your account and email record entirely, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Muscle Up isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and revise the date at the top.
